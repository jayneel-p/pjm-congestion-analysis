# Price exposure, anticipation and the size of a bet

Specified 9 October 2026 before calculation. Read the original analyzer and
renderer methods and textbook uncertainty memory first. This extension reads
SQLite only; it does not change any measurement or infer a physical branch.

The physical question is whether a condition that accompanies binding also
changes the exact price a trader would receive. The rival is that the relevant
risk already appears in DA prices, or affects a local bus while the proposed
hub contract pays something else. A useful falsifier is no incremental spread,
a change of sign, or a larger adverse block than the proposed loss budget.

Use only the paired multi-node price feed. Pair locations by UTC, verify EPT,
keep repeated autumn hours and all clock-change dates for price-only blocks.
Use the retained published holiday calendar and starts 07–22 on nonholiday
weekdays for peak; all other hours are off-peak. A is BGE_RESID_AGG minus
WESTERN HUB peak; B is DOMINION HUB minus WESTERN HUB peak; B-local is fixed
ACCA minus DOMINION HUB peak; C is COMED_RESID_AGG minus WESTERN HUB off-peak.
A is a proxy for BGE Zone and B-local is a diagnostic, not a verified ticket.

Retain hourly, daily and monthly means for total and congestion DA/RT prices.
Monthly means weight physical covered hours. Daily means are an additional
descriptive distribution, not monthly settlement scenarios. Price-only means
must reproduce the earlier complete-block means. Join anonymous occurrence
only on eligible source labels, excluding all five documented clock dates.
Calculate RT spread minus DA spread on the same hours, by recorded status and
year. This is a realized market difference, not a virtual-trade backtest.

Within 2026 January–August, show the monthly price paths and all eight monthly
outcomes, the hourly/daily empirical distributions, and exact tail contributions.
Leave negative observations and extremes intact. Quantiles describe historical
variation. No normal fit, independent-hour confidence interval or future odds.
For a mean historical RT-minus-DA difference, resample complete calendar weeks
599 times with a fixed seed; show whole-day and 14-day sensitivity. These are
retrospective mean ranges, not predicted payoff ranges. Missing nonbinding
shadows remain missing.

The trade rule uses an estimated complete-block settlement S, executable quote
q and costs c. Long profit is S-q-c; short profit is q-S-c. A buyer needs
q < E[S]-c; a seller needs q > E[S]+c. Historical monthly outcomes illustrate
quote sensitivity without assigning them future probability. Worst observed
loss is a stress example, not a bound. Position size must use covered hours and
an explicit affordable loss, and preserve contractual location/market/basis.

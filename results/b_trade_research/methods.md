# New B trade possibilities

Written 9 October 2026 after exploratory reading and before building saved
results. The question is whether B's stronger local RT than DA price response
supports a different exposure from a Dominion/Western monthly DA hub short.
The rival is that binding-selected evidence disappears at eligible trade nodes,
over complete selected calendar hours, after costs, or outside the original
regime. New trade designs are exploratory and the studied years are not fresh
holdouts. No entry, order or real quote is acquired or executed.

The principal candidate is long local RT-minus-DA basis relative to Dominion
Hub. Its per-MWh price arithmetic is (local RT minus hub RT) minus (local DA
minus hub DA). Virtual demand/decrement at an eligible local location and matched
virtual supply/increment at the hub produce that gross price exposure if both
clear for equal MW in the same hours. INC/DEC uplift, fees, partial clearing,
credit and five-minute settlement conventions remain separate. An OTC swap
paying the exact exposure is an unverified alternative, not a listed product.

Read the canonical paired multi-node hourly price feed only, with UTC/entity
keys, retaining all price measurements including negative prices and tails.
Use the existing calendar of nonholiday weekdays. The fixed primary window is
EPT hour starts 10 through 15, chosen from B's previously documented timing
before these saved calculations. It is six hours, not a full peak ICE product.
The three reference nodes ACCA/BASIN/BELLWOOD remain separately reported; none
is identified as B's endpoint. DOM_RESID_AGG is a separate proxy with its own
calculation, never silently substituted for an individual bus.

Compare January–August in 2024/2025/2026. Report hourly means, medians, positive
fractions, quantiles and extremes, plus six-hour day means, annual means and
monthly means. Resample complete calendar weeks jointly 799 times for historical
mean sensitivities, with 14-day sensitivity. These ranges omit specification
search, future regime risk, clearing and costs. Preserve every eligible day and
require all six hours and every reported price location for common support.

For transparent cost sensitivity, subtract invented remaining costs of 0, 1
and 3 dollars/MWh. They are not observed uplift or actual desk costs. A static
always-long rule and a delayed-price persistence screen are diagnostic rivals,
not demonstrated strategies. The screen uses the mean of complete prior daily
basis observations in a 28-calendar-day window ending seven days before delivery,
requiring ten earlier days. It takes a long when that prior mean exceeds an
invented 1 dollar/MWh hurdle. Evaluate 2025/2026, retain all signal/no-signal dates,
and never condition entry on realized binding, solar, output or future DA prices.
Seven-day availability of retrospective prices remains an assumption.

Also show one preset illustrative UTC bid cap of +5 dollars/MWh for the eligible
aggregate path. Accept a historical hour in this arithmetic illustration only
if its DA sink-minus-source spread does not exceed that cap. The cap is fixed
before this saved calculation, not optimized. This is a conditional auction bid,
not a claim that DA prices were known before entry. The exercise assumes full
clearing at recorded prices with no market impact and omits constraints on actual
cleared volume; it is not an execution backtest. Show all accepted/rejected hours,
historical outcome tails and invented 0/1/3 cost sensitivities.

The present PJM bidding-location sheet is a rule/eligibility snapshot, not a
historical price input or evidence that a node was eligible throughout 2024–26.
Verify exact IDs and names from its full list, retain retrieval provenance and
test the eligible aggregate's price behavior. Current Manual 11 supplies virtual
market mechanics and eligibility; Manual 28 supplies settlement and uplift

hours, counterparty quote and terms.
